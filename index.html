01. About

I am Yuqing (Jacky) Jiang, a Computer Science & Statistics undergraduate at the University of Illinois Urbana-Champaign (class of 2027). I build data, machine-learning, and quantitative systems — currently as a Quantitative Finance AI Intern at the Pacific Financial Professional Association, where I work on market-data pipelines powering a Merton/KMV structural credit-risk model.

That work sits on a broader base: five internships across fintech, analytics, and operations — at the Walt Disney Company, DP World, SeeU International, Earnest Agriculture, and PFPA — with projects spanning experimentation and causal ML, demand forecasting, and full-stack analytics platforms.

I try to bring research-grade rigor to applied work. I co-authored an ICLR 2026 workshop paper on efficient LLM adaptation, and I build real-time speech-accessibility features for ScribeAR, an open-source captioning platform used by deaf and hard-of-hearing students. Outside of work: baseball, tennis, astronomy, photography, and music.
