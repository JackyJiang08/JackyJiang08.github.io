# Map asset licenses

## world-composite.svg (generated)

Generated by `scripts/build-map.mjs` from the Natural Earth data vendored in
`data/maps-src/` — all layers share one projection (Natural Earth 1), so
country and admin-1 borders align at any zoom.

### Source data (data/maps-src/)

- `admin0-countries-50m.json` — Natural Earth 1:50m Admin 0 – Countries.
- `admin1-us-50m.json` — Natural Earth 1:50m Admin 1 – States/Provinces,
  filtered to the United States.
- `admin1-cn-10m.json` — Natural Earth 1:10m Admin 1 – States/Provinces,
  filtered to China (the 1:50m admin-1 dataset does not cover China), with
  points thinned to ~1:50m density.
- Provenance: [Natural Earth](https://www.naturalearthdata.com/) —
  **public domain** ("Free of known copyright restrictions"). Fetched via the
  [martynafford/natural-earth-geojson](https://github.com/martynafford/natural-earth-geojson)
  GeoJSON conversion.
- Modifications: properties pruned to `iso_a2`/`name`; coordinates rounded;
  China geometry point-thinned as noted above.
